লাল-পাড় সাদা — the palette of home

A probasi bangali identity for Magarpatta City: pure-white grounds, a four-step red ramp anchored on jaba #D70000, genda and shiuli accents, terracotta earth — with alpona drawn the authentic way, white line-work on red.

Alpona, drawn the right way

Alpona is white rice-paste line-work on a red or earthen ground — so on this site it lives as thin white SVG strokes on jaba bands: the page header, section dividers, event page mastheads. Motifs: podmo (lotus), kalka (paisley), maach (fish — shubho!), dhaner shish (paddy).

Rules that keep it tasteful

Red leads, alone. Jaba is the only bold voice on a screen. Sindoor, genda and shiuli appear in small doses — a hover, a chip, a garland motif — never as large fills next to the primary red.
Yellow is never text. Genda fails contrast on cream. It carries meaning as a fill behind dark ink (like the active event chip), never as lettering.
Blue and green are workers, not decoration. Durba green means one thing everywhere: success — a paid pledge, a settled claim, money in. Aparajita blue marks selection and information. Neither ever fills a hero or fights jaba for attention.
Alpona is placed, not sprinkled. Header band, section dividers, event mastheads, festive empty-states. Body screens stay quiet so the accounting tables stay readable.
Warm everywhere. No pure black, no cool greys. Text is kali ink, borders lean cream — the whole app sits in the laal-paar shada world.
Type carries both scripts. Noto Serif Bengali for display headings (আমাদের পূজা and “Time Table” alike), Hind Siliguri for UI and body. Both free on Google Fonts. This preview approximates them with system faces.
Dark mode is dhunuchi night. Smoky deep brown, brightened jaba and genda, cream ink — festive at night, not a generic grey inversion.
